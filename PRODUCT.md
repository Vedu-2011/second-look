# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Older adults managing several medicines, and adult children or caregivers helping them, often on a phone in a pharmacy or at home.

## Product Purpose
Second Look reads a pharmacy label and one pill, then compares the pill against real FDA records. It helps people catch addressable dispensing mismatches, while keeping the final verdict deterministic and showing its evidence.

## Brand Personality
Calm, exact, humane. The voice is plain and reassuring without pretending to diagnose.

## Anti-references
Not a generic health-app dashboard, a marketing hero, a blue-gradient AI demo, or an authoritative medical diagnosis.

## Design Principles
- Show the evidence behind every verdict.
- Make a worried person’s next action obvious.
- Separate AI reading from deterministic decisions.
- Design for low vision, one-handed phone use, and color-blind interpretation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG AA contrast, 18px minimum body text on mobile, large tap targets, visible keyboard focus, reduced-motion support, and icons plus words for verdict states.
